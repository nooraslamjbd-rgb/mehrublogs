AI Chatbots for Small Business: Cost & Benefits

You don't need a big budget or a tech team to have an AI chatbot. Small businesses across Pakistan are using chatbots on their websites and WhatsApp to answer questions, capture leads, and take bookings — 24 hours a day, while they sleep.

What a Chatbot Can Do For You

  • Answer common questions instantly — pricing, hours, location, services
  • Capture leads with name and phone before a human picks up
  • Qualify visitors and route hot leads straight to WhatsApp
  • Take bookings and appointments without staff
  • Recover abandoned carts on ecommerce stores
  • Provide support in English and Urdu

How Much Does a Chatbot Cost?

In Pakistan, a professionally built and trained chatbot typically costs PKR 20,000–60,000 to set up, depending on complexity. Monthly upkeep is minimal — mostly retraining on new questions and reviewing conversations. Compare that to the salary of a full-time support person and the ROI is obvious.

Website vs WhatsApp Chatbot

A website chatbot catches visitors who are browsing your site. A WhatsApp chatbot meets your customers where they already chat — crucial in Pakistan, where WhatsApp is the #1 business channel. Most businesses benefit from both: the website bot qualifies, the WhatsApp bot closes.

Will It Sound Robotic?

Modern AI chatbots with good training sound natural and on-brand. The key is training them on YOUR business — your services, prices, and tone — and setting up a smooth handoff to a human for complex conversations.
