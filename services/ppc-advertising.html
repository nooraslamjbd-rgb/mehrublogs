Overview

What Is PPC Advertising?

PPC advertising is a digital marketing model where advertisers pay each time a user clicks on their ad. It includes search ads on Google and Bing, shopping ads for ecommerce, display advertising across websites, social media ads on platforms like Facebook and LinkedIn, and retargeting campaigns that re-engage previous visitors with targeted messaging.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does PPC advertising cost?
PPC costs vary by industry, competition, and target keywords. You set your own budget and we optimize campaigns to maximize results within it. Typical monthly budgets range from a few thousand dollars for local businesses to much larger budgets for competitive industries.
How long does it take to set up a PPC campaign?
Basic campaign setup takes 1 to 2 weeks including keyword research, ad creation, landing page setup, and tracking configuration. More complex campaigns with multiple channels may require 3 to 4 weeks.
What's the difference between Google Ads and social media ads?
Google Ads targets users actively searching for your products or services with high purchase intent. Social media ads target users based on demographics, interests, and behaviors while they browse, making them better for brand awareness and consideration.
